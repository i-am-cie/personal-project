About me

I have been playing since 1.7.10. Pocket Edition at the start, Java now, PCL launcher, mods on top of everything. Roughly ten years, which is a weird thing to think about.

How it started

A classmate handed me his phone during a break and told me to try it. I could not even work the crafting table. First night I got chased across the map by a zombie and had no idea you could build a shelter.

Moved to Java because Pocket Edition had no Twilight Forest. It took me a whole evening to get PCL installed and another afternoon to figure out where mods go. I broke my install three times before it worked.

Servers

Started on Huayuting, back on Pocket Edition. Played a few hundred rounds of Bed Wars there and got to Diamond. Then Hypixel, where the ping ruined me. I would click and half a second later the other player would have already killed me.

I barely touch servers now. Sometimes Sky Wars on EaseCation with a friend. My hands are slower than they were and single-player does not require anyone to be good.

Things that happened

  • First dragon: brought a stack of bows, panicked when it flew at me, missed basically everything, then punched it to death. Nobody believes that.
  • Castle with a hole: spent four days on it. Logged in and a creeper had opened the north wall. My front door has had torches around it ever since.
  • 100 days of zombies: made it to day 40, went out at night for food, got cornered. Deleted the world instead of rebuilding.
  • A friend's villa: he promised a huge villa. It was a dirt box with a second floor. We spent half an hour laughing at it.

People I watch

On Bilibili: KamuSaMa, Mario Hongshu, Luobo Chi Miluo. I have seen every episode of their multiplayer survival series. Offical-Bean rebuilding his hometown block by block is on another level and I do not know how he has the patience.

On Douyin mostly short comedy stuff. The "100 players hunt 7 creators" videos are better planned than they look.

About this site

Hand-written HTML and CSS, no framework. It is on GitHub and published with GitHub Pages. It started as a course assignment. Then I decided if I was going to build it anyway I should make something I would actually use.
