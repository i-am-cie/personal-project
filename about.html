About this old player

I have been playing since 1.7.10, which is close to ten years now. I started on the Chinese Pocket Edition, moved to Java later, and now I run the PCL launcher with mods and modpacks installed on top.

How it started

A classmate dragged me into it — Pocket Edition, on a phone. I could not even craft a workbench at first, and on the very first night a zombie chased me across half the map. Bit by bit I learned the game, started playing survival, then found mods, and never came back.

I switched to Java because the mod selection on Pocket Edition was too thin — I could not even find Twilight Forest. It took me a whole evening to get PCL running, and another afternoon to understand how to install mods. Looking back, I knew nothing at all.

Servers I played on

I started on Huayuting, a Pocket Edition server, where I played a few hundred rounds of Bed Wars and reached Diamond. Then I tried Hypixel, but the latency was too high — in Bed Wars I was always half a second behind and got destroyed every time.

I barely play on servers now. Occasionally I join a friend for Sky Wars on EaseCation. My hands are not what they used to be, and single-player survival is simply more relaxing.

Things that happened

  • The first dragon fight — I brought a stack of bows, then panicked when the dragon came at me and missed almost every shot. In the end I killed it with my fists. Nobody believes me.
  • Half-built castle — I spent days on a large castle, logged in the next day and found a creeper had blown a hole in it. My front door has been covered in torches ever since.
  • 100 Days of Zombies — I survived 40 days, went out at night for food, and got cornered by a pack of zombies. I deleted the world and did not go back.
  • Playing with a friend — he told me he had built a huge villa. I walked over and found a stretched-out dirt box. We laughed for half an hour.

Creators I follow

On Bilibili I follow a few Minecraft creators — KamuSaMa, MarioHongshu and LuoboChiMiluo. I watch every episode of their multiplayer survival series. And Offical-Bean, who recreates his hometown block by block, is on another level.

On Douyin I mostly watch short comedy videos, like “100 players hunt 7 creators”, produced by Fangkuaizhipianju. The planning behind those is genuinely good.

How this site is made

Hand-written HTML and CSS, no framework. The code lives on GitHub and is published with GitHub Pages. It started as a course assignment; then I decided that if I was going to build it anyway, I would make something I actually want to use.
