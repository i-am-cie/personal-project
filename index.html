Ten years of Minecraft, written down

I started on 1.7 and never really stopped. Survival, building and mods — I have played all of it. This is where I keep the mistakes I made, the mods that are actually worth installing, and the castles I somehow built.

Playing right now

Update 26.60

The new ice cave biome just landed and the frostbite mechanic is genuinely fun. Frostbite zombies throw ice balls at you, and if you stand in powder snow long enough even an ordinary zombie turns.

Twilight Forest 4.9

Updated to 4.9.3850 last month, with villages. Combined with the Twilight Forest Epicfied add-on you get real Epic Fight combos instead of standing still and swinging.

A new world

I am starting a fresh survival world on 26.60. The plan: beat every Twilight Forest boss first, then come back home and build the castle.
