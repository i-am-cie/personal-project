Ten years of Minecraft, written down

I started on 1.7 and never really stopped. Survival, building, mods, a few servers. This is where I write down what worked, what wasted my time, and the castles I built out of blocks I mined myself.

Playing right now

Ice caves

The new ice cave biome is in. Frostbite zombies throw ice balls at you, and stand in powder snow long enough and a normal zombie turns into one. Lost a world to that last week.

Twilight Forest 4.9

4.9.3850 came out last month with villages. Add Epicfied and the boss fights turn into actual combos instead of standing there swinging.

New world

Fresh survival world. Plan is to kill every Twilight Forest boss first, then come home and build the castle. Same plan as last time, which I gave up at the third boss.
