Builds

All built in survival mode with materials I mined myself — not a creative-mode sandbox. Every block was carried here.

What I learned about building

  • Lay out the ground plan first — place blocks on the floor to mark the outline and check the size before building up. I once started straight away, noticed halfway that the walls were crooked, and tore it all down.
  • Never use one material everywhere — cobblestone with oak, stone bricks with oak planks; the layering is what makes it look built instead of boxed. One material genuinely looks like a dirt box.
  • Towers are the soul — any castle, big or small, instantly looks like a castle once it has a few towers. My first build only started working after I added two.
  • Do not skimp on lighting — torches, lanterns, glowstone. You only see the dark corners at night, so I now do a full lap after finishing.
  • Details take the longest — windows, door frames, a garden. Small things cost more time than big structures; the castle has been “almost done” for a month.
