Builds

All three were built in survival. No creative mode, no world edit. Every block came down a ladder at some point.

What I learned about building

  • Draw the outline on the ground first. Blocks on the floor, walk around it, check the size. The one time I skipped this I got halfway up and the walls were crooked.
  • Use two materials, not one. Cobble with oak, stone brick with oak planks. One material everywhere is the reason people call your house a dirt box.
  • Towers do most of the work. Any box gets called a castle once it has two towers. My first build only started looking like something after I added them.
  • Light it at night, not during the day. You cannot see the dark corners in daylight. I do a lap around the outside after the sun goes down.
  • Details eat the time. Windows, door frames, a garden, a path. The big shapes take a weekend and the details take a month. Mine has been “almost done” for three weeks.
