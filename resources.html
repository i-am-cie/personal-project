Links

After ten years these are the only sites I still open. Everything else I found once and forgot.

Game and launchers

  • Minecraft.net. Buy it here. Do not download it from anywhere else.
  • CurseForge. Biggest mod and modpack library. Slow to load.
  • Modrinth. Faster, cleaner, and what I use now unless a mod is only on CurseForge.
  • PCL2 launcher. Free, made by one person, and it handles mod installs for you.

Tools

  • Chunkbase. Give it a seed and it shows strongholds, temples, biomes. I use it for every world.
  • Minecraft Seed Map. Online map viewer for a seed. Good when I want to see the terrain before spawning.
  • Slime Finder. Slime chunks. You need this before building a slime farm, otherwise you build it in the wrong place like I did.

Looking things up

  • Minecraft Wiki. Still the most complete reference, even if the ads are awful.
  • Bilibili tutorials. Search what you want to learn. Watching someone do it beats reading a guide.
  • Creeper forum. Chinese player community, good for modpack resources.

Watching

  • Bilibili, Minecraft survival series. I watch more than I play these days.
  • Douyin, short clips. Good for five minutes between things.
