Useful sites

After years of playing, these are the only sites I still open. Saves searching around every time.

Downloads and launchers

  • Minecraft.net — buy the game here. Do not download it from random sites.
  • CurseForge — the biggest mod and modpack library, though it loads slowly.
  • Modrinth — the other mod platform, faster, and what I mainly use now.
  • PCL2 launcher — a free launcher that makes installing mods easy.

Tools

  • Chunkbase — strongholds, temples and biomes from a seed; enter the coordinates and go.
  • Mapcraft — an online map viewer; upload a world and see the whole thing.
  • Slime Finder — find slime chunks, which you need for a slime farm.

Learning and looking things up

  • Minecraft Wiki — the most complete reference there is.
  • Bilibili tutorials — search for what you want to learn; faster than reading a text guide.
  • The Creeper forum — a Chinese player community with a lot of modpack resources.

Something to watch

  • Bilibili — search for Minecraft survival series; watching others play is its own kind of fun.
  • Douyin — short Minecraft comedy clips for spare minutes.
