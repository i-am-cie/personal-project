Survival notes

Most of this I learned by dying. Some of it took several worlds to stick.

Day one: stay alive

  • Get wood first. Ten logs before anything else. People run off to explore on day one and then the sun goes down and they have nothing.
  • Skip wooden tools. Wood pickaxe to get stone, then stone pickaxe and sword. A full wooden set is a waste of wood.
  • Be inside before dark. Dig three down and put a block over your head, or wall off a cave. Do not go for a night walk on day one.
  • Food can wait. Kill a sheep, grab an apple. You only need to not starve.

First three days

  • Iron changes the game. A full iron set and a bucket. I carry the bucket every day, it saves me from lava and from falls I do not see coming.
  • Make a bed as soon as you find sheep. Three sheep and you skip the whole night. A night without a bed is a coin flip.
  • Plant wheat early. Hunting every day gets old fast. Farming is boring until you are hungry.
  • Build something temporary. It does not need to look good. You will tear it down in a week anyway.

First week

  • Diamonds around Y=-58. Bring a few iron pickaxes and too many torches. I dig one long tunnel and usually come back with a stack.
  • Enchanting table. Once the diamond armour is enchanted the game gets noticeably easier. Fortune and Efficiency are the two that matter most.
  • Farms. Wheat, sugar cane, melons, then automate them. My first automatic farm took two days and half of it did not work.
  • Nether. Obsidian, portal, blaze rods. Take more blocks than you think you need. Falling in lava is not an if.

Late game

  • Stronghold. Eyes of ender, and carry spare ender pearls. My first search took three days and I ran out of eyes twice.
  • Dragon. Crystals first. Then the dragon. Do not go in empty-handed and do not forget food.
  • Build the real house. After the dragon there is finally a reason to build properly. The castle I am on now has taken about a month.
  • End cities. Elytra. After that the map stops being a map. I fell to my death on my first flight because I missed the water.

Habits

  • Water bucket in the hotbar, always. Lava, falls, mobs, all of it.
  • Ender chest for anything you cannot lose. Carry the materials for one.
  • Build near water. Farming, travel, fishing.
  • Walk around the house before sleeping. Creepers are quiet.
  • Silk Touch on a diamond pickaxe. Ice, stone, glass, everything.
