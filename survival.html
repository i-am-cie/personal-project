Survival notes

Years of survival, and more mistakes than blocks placed. All of it learned the hard way, not copied from a guide.

Day one: stay alive

  • Punch wood first — do not run off and explore. Get ten logs before anything else. I have watched too many people get surrounded on the first night without even a crafting table.
  • Go straight to stone tools — wooden pickaxe on stone, then stone pickaxe and sword. Do not waste wood on a full wooden set.
  • Be inside before dark — dig three down and cover yourself, or wall off a cave. Do not wander out on the first night. You will die.
  • Do not worry about food yet — eat whatever you find on day one: a couple of sheep, an apple. Just do not starve.

First three days: get a foothold

  • Iron changes everything — a full iron set and a bucket double your quality of life. I carry a water bucket every single day; it saves me from lava and from falls.
  • Make a bed — find three sheep and sleep through the monster wave. A night without a bed is a night spent gambling.
  • Plant wheat — solve food for the long run instead of hunting every day. I am honestly better at farming than at fighting now.
  • Build a temporary shelter — it does not need to look good, it just needs to keep monsters out. You will rebuild later anyway.

First week: build up

  • Mine diamonds — around Y = -58 is where they turn up most often. Bring enough iron pickaxes and torches. I dig one long tunnel and usually fill a stack in half a day.
  • Enchanting table — once your diamond gear is enchanted, survival becomes noticeably easier. Fortune plus Efficiency makes mining and farming both pleasant.
  • Start farms — wheat, sugar cane, melons, and slowly automate them. My first automatic farm took me two full days.
  • Go to the Nether — mine obsidian, build the portal, find blazes for rods. Bring plenty of blocks on the first trip and stay away from lava.

Late game: the dragon and a real home

  • Find a stronghold — locate it with eyes of ender and bring enough ender pearls. My first search took three days and I ran out of eyes twice.
  • Fight the dragon — shoot the crystals first, then deal with the dragon. Bring arrows and food; do not go in empty-handed.
  • Build the real house — after the dragon, it is finally time to build properly. The castle I am working on now has taken almost a month.
  • Explore end cities — elytra is out there, and after you have it the map is yours. I fell to my death on the first flight because I missed the water landing.

Small habits of an old player

  • Carry a water bucket — lava, falls, clearing mobs, all in one slot. Mine never leaves my hotbar.
  • Keep valuables in an ender chest — dying then costs you nothing. I always carry the materials for one.
  • Build near water — farming, travel and fishing all get easier. All three of my main worlds sit by a river.
  • Check your surroundings before sleeping — do not get woken up by a creeper. I do one lap around the house every night now.
  • Silk Touch on a diamond pickaxe — ice, stone, glass, whatever. It is the late-game quality-of-life enchantment.
